# Daily Finance Audio Content Spec

Audience: user has basic finance knowledge and wants practical, rigorous explanations for research reading, modeling, interviews, and investment judgment.

Style: patient university lecture. Clear, rigorous, steady, and lightly conversational.

Length: 1100 to 1500 Chinese characters. Target 5 minutes, upper bound 6.5 minutes.

Structure:
1. Previous review, 20 to 30 seconds.
2. Today's question, 15 to 25 seconds.
3. Concept definition, 60 to 90 seconds.
4. Mechanism breakdown, 90 to 150 seconds.
5. Two examples, 90 to 150 seconds.
6. Common misconception, 40 to 60 seconds.
7. Practical takeaway, 20 to 30 seconds.
8. Tomorrow's review question, 10 to 20 seconds.

Term rule: introduce each English finance term once with a Chinese explanation, then reuse the English term directly.

Formula rule: explain formulas verbally only when they help the intuition.

Example rule: use no more than two main examples per episode.

Style guard: avoid empty wording, tables, dense brackets, unexplained abbreviation clusters, the user's banned comparison sentence pattern, and dash punctuation used as a break.
